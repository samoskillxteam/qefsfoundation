Who We Are

A Humanitarian Mission Born from Pain, Sustained by Grace

Queen Ecstasy Family Support Foundation is a non-profit humanitarian organization based in Abuja, Nigeria, established with a passion for supporting young widows, vulnerable families, and children. Through strategic partnerships, skills training, scholarships, and food relief, we help families rebuild their lives with dignity.

Founder's Divine Calling

The Story Behind Our Foundation

"At age 24, I became a young widow with two children to care for. I went through deep pain and uncertainty, but God's grace carried me, restored my home with a loving husband, and blessed us with twins. This calling was born from pain, sustained by grace, and driven by love to help every young widow believe in tomorrow again."

Empathy from Lived Experience

We know firsthand the trauma of early widowhood and what a compassionate hand can do when someone is almost giving up.

From Mourning to Sustainable Dancing

Our goal is not just temporary relief, but long-term vocational independence, child education, and household stability.
