Our Story & Purpose

A Humanitarian Mission Born from Pain, Sustained by Grace

"At the age of 24, I became a young widow with two children to raise. The world seemed crushed beneath my feet with grief, judgment, and acute uncertainty. But God's grace carried me through, restored my joy, blessed me with a loving husband, and gave us beautiful twins. That divine experience birthed this burden: that no young widow should ever walk through darkness alone or believe their story is over."
— Queen Esther Chukwumaeze, Founder

Queen Ecstasy Family Support Foundation is a registered non-governmental organization headquartered in Kubwa, Abuja, Nigeria, actively reaching and uplifting vulnerable populations across Abuja and Imo State communities. We are passionately committed to restoring young widows to economic independence, providing fatherless and indigent children with quality education scholarships, and feeding struggling households through targeted humanitarian outreaches.

Our Mission

To empower young widows and vulnerable families through skills acquisition, child scholarships, psychosocial healing, and relief outreach, restoring their hope and dignity.

Our Vision

A nation where every young widow is respected, self-sufficient, and economically stable, and every vulnerable child has access to an uninterrupted quality education.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

These fundamental tenets guide our daily humanitarian operations, stewardship of donor funds, and community interactions across Abuja and Imo State.

Compassion & Empathy

We treat every widow and child with profound love, respect, and emotional gentleness, understanding the pain of loss and hardship.

Integrity & Transparency

Every naira and donor contribution is deployed directly to verified beneficiaries with rigorous accountability and transparent stewardship.

Sustainable Empowerment

Beyond temporary handouts, we believe in equipping mothers with marketable vocational skills and startup capital to build long-term independence.

Faith & Resilience

Inspired by divine grace, we instill unshakeable hope in despairing families, helping them rise above tragedy into joy and restoration.

Community & Inclusivity

We assist all vulnerable widows and orphans irrespective of their ethnic, tribal, or religious background across Abuja, Imo State, and surrounding regions.

Humanitarian Excellence

We conduct our outreaches with the highest degree of professionalism, dignity for recipients, and measurable community outcomes.
