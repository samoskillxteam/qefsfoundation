Comprehensive Human Support

Empowering Families from Vulnerability to Victory

Our structured programs target root causes of vulnerability, providing skills, education, emergency food parcels, and health interventions to young widows and children across Nigeria.

Young Widows Vocational Skills

Practical training in fashion tailoring, baking, soap making, and craft production, paired with startup sewing machines and seed capital grants so widows can provide for their children with dignity.

  • Free 3-month certified vocational courses
  • Provision of sewing machines & toolkits
  • Micro-business financial literacy training
Sponsor a Widow

Orphan & Child Scholarships

Ensuring children left fatherless or from indigent families continue schooling unhindered. We cover primary and secondary school tuition, school uniforms, textbooks, and backpacks.

  • Full annual tuition & exam fees
  • Back-to-school bags & stationary packs
  • After-school academic tutoring & mentorship
Sponsor a Child

Emergency Food Relief

Direct household food packages distributed on a scheduled basis to young widows caring for toddlers and orphans, preventing acute malnutrition and hunger crises.

  • 25kg & 50kg bags of rice, beans, & garri
  • Vegetable cooking oil & essential seasonings
  • Nutritional supplements for infants & toddlers
Donate Food Packs

Healthcare & Medical Outreaches

Mobile medical camps organized in Abuja suburbs offering free diagnosis, doctor consultations, vital checks, prescription drugs, and maternal health education.

  • Free blood pressure & diabetes screenings
  • Malaria treatments & preventative mosquito nets
  • Maternal & post-natal healthcare guidance
Support Health Mission

Counseling & Psycho-Social Healing

Grief counseling, peer support sisterhood circles, spiritual mentorship, and mental health assistance led by compassionate facilitators to heal broken hearts.

  • Monthly widows fellowship & grief sharing
  • One-on-one trauma therapy sessions
  • Faith-based encouragement & prayer support
Join Support Circle

Legal Rights Advocacy for Widows

Protecting young Nigerian widows from harmful cultural practices, forced disinheritance, eviction, and harassment, ensuring their children keep their inheritance.

  • Pro-bono legal counsel and mediation
  • Property & asset rights protection
  • Community awareness & rights seminars
